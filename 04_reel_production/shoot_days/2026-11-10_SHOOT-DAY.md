## 2. Set-up (20 min)
1. [ ] **Room:** all tube lights ON. Fan OFF. AC OFF if it's noisy. Close the door. Phones on silent.
2. [ ] **Shawn's mark:** stand where the Day 1 video was filmed, with the 3D printer **about 1 m behind you, on your LEFT**. Put a **cross of tape on the floor** between your feet.
3. [ ] **Tripod:** put it **1.1 m in front of the tape mark** (measure from the mark to the tripod's centre). Tape around the tripod feet too, so you can put it back after the B-roll.
4. [ ] **iPhone settings** (Settings → Camera): Record Video = **4K at 30 fps** · **HDR Video OFF** · Formats = Most Compatible · Grid ON. In the Camera app: **Video** mode, **1×** lens (not 0.5×), **phone vertical**.
5. [ ] **Height:** raise the tripod until the iPhone lens is **level with Shawn's eyes** while he stands on the mark.
6. [ ] **Frame check** (Shawn on the mark): the bottom edge of the picture is at his **belt**, there's **one hand-width of space above his head**, and the printer shows on the **right side of the picture**. Tilt/raise the tripod until all three are true.
7. [ ] **Light check:** on the iPhone screen, no dark shadows under Shawn's eyes. If there are, Shawn takes half a step toward the camera so the nearest tube is in front of him (move the tape mark too).
8. [ ] **Mic:** transmitter clipped on the T-shirt collar, **20 cm (one hand-width) below the chin**, facing up. Receiver plugged into the iPhone.
9. [ ] **Teleprompter:** stand the second phone upright on a box/books directly under the iPhone, top edge touching the bottom of the iPhone. Open the LAUNCH1 note at "B1".
10. [ ] **Lock:** tap and hold on Shawn's face on the iPhone screen until **AE/AF LOCK** shows.
11. [ ] **Test:** record 5 s of Shawn talking. Play it back **with earphones**: is the voice clear, without hiss or wind? Is the face sharp? Delete the test.
12. [ ] **Start the 3D print** now, so it's printing during LAUNCH1.

## 3. How to record every piece (same routine every time)
1. Helper says the piece name ("LAUNCH1, B1, take 1") and **writes it in the take log** (step 7).
2. Helper presses **record** on the iPhone.
3. Shawn **counts 2 seconds silently**, glances at his line on the teleprompter, **looks up into the iPhone lens**, says it with the action from the script.
   - **Takes 1 and 2:** his own written line, close to what's on the teleprompter.
   - **Last take of each piece:** **free take**. Say the same meaning as if explaining to a friend; it can be a bit longer, but **no new points and no new facts**.
   - The best of these is picked in the edit.
4. Shawn **holds still for 2 seconds** after the last word.
5. Helper presses **stop**. One recording = one piece = one file.
6. Mistake? Stop, and start that piece again as the next take. Don't fix it mid-file.
7. After the last take of a piece, the helper says which take felt best and **circles it** in the log, then swipes the teleprompter to the next piece.
