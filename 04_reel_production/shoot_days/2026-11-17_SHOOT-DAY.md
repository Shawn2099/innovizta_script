## 3. How to record every piece (same routine as 10 Nov)
1. Helper says "LAUNCH3, B1, take 1" and writes the row in the take log.
2. Helper presses record. Shawn counts 2 s silently, glances at his line, looks into the lens and says it with the action.
3. Shawn holds still 2 s after the last word. Helper presses stop. **One recording = one piece.**
4. **Takes 1–2:** his written line. **Last take:** free take (same meaning, like explaining to a friend, no new facts).
5. Mistake? Stop and start again as the next take.
