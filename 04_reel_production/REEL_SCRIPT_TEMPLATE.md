# Global Reel Script Template · The Project Shop
v1 · 6 Nov 2026 · for Shawn (host) and the editor (Claude + `pipeline/`)
**Every reel script is a copy of Part D, filled in using the codes from Parts A–C. If something isn't coded, it isn't in the reel.**
Based on: `REEL_FORMULA.md` (26 competitor reels) + production-craft research. Numbers marked *(test)* are best-practice defaults to confirm on our own reels.

## PART A: Fixed production codes (set up once, reuse every shoot)

### A1. Sets (SET)
| Code | Where | Background | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SET-OFF** | Office desk | Shawn 1.5–2 m in front of the shelf/wall with logo + a lit practical lamp. Tidy, nothing moving | Talking head (Survival, Drop, Story) |
| **SET-WS** | Workshop bench | 3D printer / tools / PCBs visible behind or beside | Project builds, AI/tool, Human fails |
| **SET-OUT** | Outdoors (campus-like spot, Infopark, town) | Open shade or golden hour, sun behind the camera or to the side, never noon | Variety, local signal (location tag) |
| **SET-SCR** | Screen capture / phone screen | Clean desktop, notifications OFF | Listings, tools, diagrams |

### A2. Camera positions (CAM)
| Code | Position | Height / distance | Lens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CAM-A** | Front, straight-on (main) | Lens at Shawn's eye level · 1.0–1.2 m | Main 1× (never ultra-wide), 4K 30 fps, exposure + focus + white balance **locked** |
| **CAM-B** | 30° to Shawn's left, eyes still to CAM-A's lens or just off | Same height · 1.2 m | Same settings, second pass for key lines only |
| **CAM-C** | Close-up / insert of hands or object | 30–50 cm, slightly above, tripod or handheld steady | 1× or 2× |
| **CAM-D** | Overhead / top-down on the bench | Arm or tripod over the desk | 1× |
| **CAM-POV** | Handheld point-of-view (Shawn holding the phone) | Chest height | 1×, slow movement |

### A3. Frame sizes (FR)
| Code | Frame | When |
|---|---|---|
| **FR-CU** | Forehead/top of head to upper chest. Eyes on the upper-third line | Hook, punchlines, CTA |
| **FR-MCU** | Head to mid-chest (**default**) | Explaining |
| **FR-MS** | Head to waist, hands visible | Demos, holding props, gestures |
| **FR-INS** | Object only (from CAM-C/D) | Builds, components, results |
| **FR-SCR** | Full-screen screen capture | Listings, tools |
*Punch-ins are made in the edit by cropping the 4K: MCU → CU = 115–125 %.*

### A3b. Framing guide in plain English (for Shawn: "how much of me is in the shot?")
| Name we use | What's in the frame | Camera | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Face shot** | top of head to upper chest | close, or the editor zooms in from a waist-up shot | hook, punchline, ending |
| **Waist-up** (default) | head to waist, hands visible | front, 1.0–1.2 m, eye level | **all talking pieces** (the editor zooms in from this) |
| **Sitting upper body** | head to desk top, hands on the desk | front, lens at sitting eye level | desk/laptop reels |
| **Full body** | head to feet | 2.5–3 m away, wide | workshop wide shots, walking B-roll |
| **From behind** | back of head + shoulders, or full body from behind | 1.5–3 m behind Shawn | "working" / walking B-roll |
| **Over the shoulder** | Shawn's shoulder + back of head in one corner, the screen/board he's looking at in the rest | 1 m behind, to one side, a bit higher than his head | laptop / board B-roll |
| **Hands only** | hands + the object, no face | close, 30–50 cm | building, writing, flipping, pointing |
| **Top-down** | the desk from directly above (hands + paper/board) | phone held flat above the desk, 40–60 cm | notebooks, plans, parts |
| **Object close-up** | just the thing (printer nozzle, sensor, screen) | 15–30 cm, static | proof shots |
| **Screen recording** | phone/laptop screen only | built-in screen recorder | chats, listings, documents |
**Rule:** talking = one fixed waist-up (or sitting) frame for the whole reel. Every other frame is B-roll, filmed after the talking pieces and listed in the script's SHOT LIST.

### A4. Posture and action (POS)
| Code | Body | Hands | Eyes | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **POS-HOOK** | Lean in ~10 cm, shoulders square | One gesture or the prop raised into frame | Lens | **High** (+20 % above normal) |
| **POS-EXPL** | Upright, relaxed, camera-side shoulder slightly open | Gesture zone (chest–chin), inside frame | Lens | Medium-high |
| **POS-DEMO** | Slight turn toward the object | Hold the object **still for 1–2 s** at chest height, facing the lens | Object → lens | Medium |
| **POS-POINT** | Upright | Point up/side to where the on-screen text will appear | Lens | Medium |
| **POS-CTA** | Lean in slightly | Point **down** (to the comments) or hold up 1 finger | Lens | High, smiling |
| **POS-REACT** | Natural | Natural | Toward the object/screen | Real (laugh, sigh), not acted |

**Delivery rules (every take):** look into the lens, not the screen · **0.5 s of silence before and after every line** (clean cut points) · flub = restart the whole sentence · ~2.5–3 words/s (Manglish runs fast, so slow down slightly) · announce the object before showing it.

### A5. Light and sound (fixed)
- Key light: window or LED at **45°**, slightly above eye level. Fill/reflector on the other side. All lights the **same colour temperature**. Overhead tube lights OFF if they cause eye shadows.
- Lav mic clipped about 20 cm below the chin. Fan OFF. **Record 10 s of room tone** at every set (tag `RT-<SET>`).
- Same tee/hoodie as the series "uniform" (solid colour, no fine stripes).

## PART B: Editor codes and standards (fixed)

### B1. Canvas and safe zones
- Export **1080×1920, 9:16, 30 fps, H.264 MP4**, from 4K source.
- **Text safe zone:** keep all text **>270 px from the top, >670 px from the bottom, >160 px from the right edge**, and >60 px from the left *(test: confirm on a real phone, since sources vary)*.
- **Cover:** keep the face + title inside the **centre 1080×1350**, because the grid crops it.

### B2. Text styles (TXT). Hand-typed only, **never auto-captions**
| Code | Use | Style | Max words | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TXT-HOOK** | First 0–3 s | Bold caps, white with black outline or a black box, ~90–110 px, upper third | 8 | 0.0 s → until replaced (≥2 s) |
| **TXT-KEY** | Key line or number | Bold, ~70–80 px, brand orange `#FF8A00` highlight on 1 word | 6 | 1.5–3 s |
| **TXT-LABEL** | Name a thing ("ESP32", "₹15K/month") | Medium ~50–60 px, white on a dark pill | 4 | while visible |
| **TXT-CARD** | Info card (Drop / component list) | Template card (see the format doc) | — | 3–5 s |
| **TXT-CTA** | Final ask | Big, **keyword in orange**: `Comment "PROJECT"` | 5 | last 3–5 s |
Rule of thumb: on screen for ≥1 s, + 0.25 s per word. Animate in over 4–6 frames (pop/slide). One text block at a time.

### B3. Cuts and motion (CUT)
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **CUT-JUMP** | Remove pauses >0.25 s, breaths and "um"s, keeping 2–4 frames of handle. Never cut mid-word |
| **CUT-PUNCH** | Hard punch-in to 115–125 % on the emphasised word, or alternating 100/115 at each sentence |
| **CUT-INS** | Cut to insert/B-roll for 1–3 s the moment the thing is named (audio keeps running = J/L cut, 6–12 frames) |
| **CUT-HOLD** | Let a continuous action shot run (build/print/fail) with **no cuts** while it keeps changing (Formula rule 5) |
| **CUT-SPEED** | Speed up a slow process 2–8× (timelapse), add a small "×4" TXT-LABEL |
**Visual-change rule:** something must change every **2–4 s** on a talking head (cut / punch / text) and every **4–6 s** on action shots (the action itself counts).

### B4. Sound (SND)
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SND-VO** | Voice: target **-14 LUFS integrated**, true peak -1 dBTP *(test)* |
| **SND-BED** | Music bed ~12 dB under the voice (about -26 LUFS); duck a further 3–6 dB under key lines. Licensed/royalty-free or Instagram library only |
| **SND-WHOOSH / SND-POP / SND-HIT** | Transition / text pop / reveal hit. -18 to -12 dB, max 1 every 3–4 s. Use `build_reel.py`'s synthesized SFX |
| **SND-NAT** | Natural sound up (printer, motor, click). Often better than music on builds |

### B5. Fixed elements (every reel)
- Small logo watermark (`pipeline/assets/logo_transparent.png`), top-left inside the safe zone, 60 % opacity.
- Thin progress bar (built into build_reel.py).
- End card (last 1.5–2 s): logo + handle. **TXT-CTA keyword card only on lead reels (DROP, PIW)**. Value reels (SRV, AIB) use a spoken/caption ask only (big-page validation). **Exception:** loop reels (Human) have no end card.
- **Series signature:** each series keeps the same pill style, key-word colour, progress bar and end card, so the grid and the feed read as a set.
- Grade: white balance first, mild contrast and saturation, the same preset on every reel.

### B6. Transitions (TRN). Default = hard cut. Every transition must have a reason
| Code | What | Length | Use when | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TRN-CUT** | Hard cut (**default**) | 0 frames | Everything not listed below | none |
| **TRN-PUNCH** | Hard cut + scale jump 100→115–125 % (same take) | 0 frames | Emphasis inside one talking-head take; hides jump cuts | optional SND-POP |
| **TRN-JL** | Audio leads (J) or trails (L) the picture | audio 6–12 frames early/late | Talking head ↔ insert, so speech flows over the change | none |
| **TRN-MATCH** | Match cut: same shape/motion in both shots (hand reaches → close-up of hand) | 0 frames | Build steps, before → after | optional SND-WHOOSH |
| **TRN-WHIP** | Fast blur pan 4–6 frames between shots | 4–6 frames | Location/set change (SET-OFF → SET-WS), list item change | SND-WHOOSH |
| **TRN-ZOOMIN** | Quick 8–10 frame push into the next shot | 8–10 frames | Reveal / "look at this" | SND-WHOOSH |
| **TRN-FLASH** | 2–3 frame white flash | 2–3 frames | **Payoff moment only** (B4), max 1 per reel. Never at 0.0 s | SND-HIT |
| **TRN-SPEED** | Speed ramp 1× → 4–8× → 1× | ramp 6 frames each side | Long process (print, solder, assemble) | SND-NAT sped up, or a music swell |
| **TRN-FREEZE** | Freeze frame 0.5–1.0 s + TXT-LABEL | 0.5–1.0 s | Point at a detail ("this sensor") | SND-POP |
| **TRN-NONE (loop)** | Last frame flows into the first frame | — | F4 Human loops | — |
Banned: dissolves/crossfades on talking heads, spins, page curls, glitch packs. They read as "template" and slow the pace.

### B7. Clip-duration rules (how long each shot stays on screen)
| Shot type | Min | Typical | Max before a change | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B0 first frame | — | 0.5–1.0 s | 1.0 s | Already in action at frame 1 |
| Talking head (FR-CU/MCU), one take | 1.5 s | 2–4 s | **4 s** → then TRN-PUNCH, a text change or an insert | Count the text change as a "change" |
| Insert / B-roll (FR-INS) | 1.0 s | 1.5–3 s | 4 s | Cut in as the thing is named |
| Prop hold (POS-DEMO) | 1.0 s | 1.5–2 s | 3 s | Object still, facing the lens |
| Screen capture (FR-SCR) | 2.0 s | 3–5 s | 6 s | Long enough to read. Add a TXT-LABEL or arrow to the key number |
| Action shot (CUT-HOLD: print/fail/demo running) | 3 s | 5–15 s | as long as it keeps visibly changing | Cut the moment it stops changing |
| Timelapse (CUT-SPEED) | 2 s | 3–6 s | 8 s | Show the "×N" label |
| Payoff / reveal (B4) | 2 s | 3–4 s | 5 s | Let it breathe. SND-HIT on the first frame |
| TXT-HOOK hold | 2 s | until B2 starts | 3.5 s | |
| TXT-CARD (Drop card, component list) | 3 s | 3–5 s | 6 s | |
| End card (B6) | 1.5 s | 2 s | 2.5 s | |
**Total check:** the sum of beat durations = TARGET LENGTH ±2 s, otherwise trim B3 first, never B0, B1 or B5.

### B8. Overlays and extras (OVL). Use sparingly
| Code | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **OVL-ARROW / OVL-CIRCLE** | Hand-drawn style arrow or circle in brand orange | Point at ONE detail. Appears with SND-POP, stays 1–2 s |
| **OVL-PIP** | Shawn in a small circle (bottom-left, inside the safe zone) over a screen capture | F3/F7 screen-heavy beats, so the face stays present |
| **OVL-SPLIT** | Top/bottom split (screen over Shawn) | Only when both halves matter at the same moment; otherwise use OVL-PIP |
| **OVL-COUNT** | "1/3, 2/3, 3/3" small counter, top-right inside the safe zone | List formats (F2, F3) so viewers know how much is left |
| **OVL-PILL** | Numbered-promise pill at the top of the safe zone: "3 VIVA QUESTIONS · 2/3", changing per beat | Long lists (>40 s): the spine of the reel (Ankur pattern) |
| **OVL-EVIDENCE** | Evidence card/screenshot (price table, job post, KTU notice, component list) for 2–4 s | By 10–15 s in any claim-based reel. Real and current only, blur names |
| **OVL-PRICE** | Price/stipend tag (TXT-LABEL in orange) | Only real, current numbers |
| **OVL-BLUR** | Blur box | Any name, face, phone number or college of a client or commenter without permission |
| **MUS-SYNC** | Cuts land on the music beat | Optional for F1/F4 montages. Never let the beat override speech timing |

### B9. Cover (thumbnail)
- A frame from the reel (usually B0 or B4), or a separate shot of Shawn CU + the object.
- Text ≤4 words, TXT-HOOK style, inside the **centre 1080×1350** (grid crop).
- Same layout every time per series (PIW / SRV / DROP / AIB) so the grid looks like a set.

## PART C: Beat structure (the formula as timings)

| Beat | Name | Job | 20–30 s reel | 45 s reel | 60–90 s build/story |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B0** | First frame | One real subject already doing something + TXT-HOOK on screen at 0.0 s | 0.0–1.0 | 0.0–1.0 | 0.0–1.0 |
| **B1** | Hook | Tension line (spoken and/or text) of ≤8 words. Types: **FAIL · NOT-SIMPLE · FEAR · QUESTION · RESULT-FIRST · NAME · POV · STORY** | 1–3 | 1–3 | 1–3 |
| **B2** | Promise | Why keep watching: "ithaa 3 steps" / "last one is the trick" (open loop) | 3–6 | 3–8 | 3–10 |
| **B3** | Value | 1–3 points / build steps, each with its own visual | 6–22 | 8–35 | 10–70 |
| **B4** | Payoff | The result, the reveal, the answer to B1 | 22–26 | 35–40 | 70–80 |
| **B5** | Ask | ONE ask: keyword comment *or* send-to-friend *or* follow, spoken + TXT-CTA | 26–30 | 40–45 | 80–88 |
| **B6** | End | End card, or a loop (last line leads back into the first) | +1.5 | +1.5 | +1.5 |

**Hooks:** the hook types (FAIL · NOT-SIMPLE · FEAR · QUESTION · RESULT-FIRST · NAME · POV · STORY · NUMBERED-PROMISE · X-VS-Y · NOT-X-ITS-Y · ACCESS/PROOF · AUDIENCE-ANSWER), the 15 fill-in templates and the **12-point hook score** live in **`SCRIPT_PLAYBOOK.md` §3** (single source, don't duplicate here). Every factual claim in a hook must be true for Shawn / The Project Shop.

## PART D: The script sheet (copy this for every reel)
**Every script starts with D0, "the whole reel in plain English":** a one-sentence idea, why we make it, Shawn's role and energy, then a table per beat: **what you SAY (English meaning) · what it's for · how to ACT (simple words, no codes) · what the viewer SEES.** The Manglish lines are suggestions only. **Shawn says the English meaning in his own natural Manglish.** (Added after Shawn's feedback on LAUNCH1: the Manglish translation was poor and the coded acting directions were unclear.)

A complete script = **D0 plain-English version + D1 header + D1b control/facts + D2 hook + D3 cover + shot table + shoot list + D4 shoot card + D5 publishing pack + YAML editor block**, and later D6 results. If any block is empty, the script isn't ready to shoot.

### D4b. READ MODE: the teleprompter script (default way to record)
Shawn doesn't need to memorise anything. He reads each line from a **teleprompter on a second phone/tablet placed directly under the camera lens** (any free teleprompter app, scroll speed off, one line per screen), looks into the lens and says it. **One beat = one recording file.**
```
FILE NAME (say nothing extra, just record): <REEL NAME>_<beat>_t<take>.mp4   e.g. IDEA1_B1_t2.mp4
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
B1  [POS-HOOK: lean in, hold the ESP32 next to your face, eyebrows up]
    "Viva-yil 'ithu engane work aakunnu' chodichaal… answer undo?"
    → 5 takes · pause 1 s before and after
B2  [POS-EXPL: sit back, relaxed]
    "Panel moonnu chodyangal eppozhum chodikkum."   [pause]
    "Moonnamathe aanu ellarum fail aakunnath."
    → 3 takes
B3.1 [POS-POINT: 1 finger up]
    "Onnu: ithinte block diagram explain cheyyaamo?"
...
B5  [POS-CTA: lean in, smile, point down]
    "Ninte project partner-nu ithu send cheyyu."
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```
Rules for writing read-mode lines:
- **≤12 words per line, one breath, one idea.** Long thoughts get split into 2 lines with [pause] between them
- Every line starts with a **[direction cue]** (posture, hands, face, prop), so Shawn never has to decide anything
- Write the words exactly as spoken (Manglish in Latin script), with technical terms in English
- Mark emphasis words in CAPS (Shawn hits them; the editor punches in there)
- **Hook and CTA lines are word-for-word**. Middle lines may be read naturally
- Shawn reads the line, **looks up at the lens, then says it**. Never talk while looking down at the prompter
- After the last take of each beat: say nothing, stop, and start the next file (clean files = fast, accurate editing)
- Mark your best take by saying "**keep**" right after it, or by renaming it `_KEEP`. Otherwise the editor uses the last take

## PART F: Shoot-day rules
1. Shoot **in shot-table order per set** (all SET-OFF beats, then SET-WS, …), not in reel order.
2. **Slate every take:** say "B3.2, take 2" out loud (or show fingers) before the line. The editor uses it to find takes.
3. Hook: 5 takes with different energy. Other beats: 2–3 takes.
4. Leave the camera rolling on the bench during builds, because fails make the best F4 reels.
5. Record room tone at every set. Log any problem in a `shoot_notes.txt` next to the files.
6. File naming: `<shoot date YYYY-MM-DD>/<REEL NAME>_<beat>_t<take>.mp4`, or one long file per set plus slates.
