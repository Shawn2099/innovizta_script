## 1. The 15 laws (each backed by at least 2 sources; the skills overlap with blog material, so treat them as one family)
| # | Law | Backed by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Frame 1 shows a real thing already happening.** No greeting, no logo, don't start with "Njan…" (unless it's a STORY reel) | Our data (rule 1), MrBeast guide, reels-scripting |
| 2 | **Hook = visual + on-screen text + first spoken line, all saying the same thing**, landed by 3 s and understandable on mute | MrBeast guide, hook-writer, our data (rule 2) |
| 3 | **Tension, not a topic label.** Open a gap: fail, "not as simple as it looks", fear, question, stakes, specific number | Our data (rule 3), Kane, Kallaway, Made to Stick (unexpected) |
| 4 | **One idea per reel**, concrete and visible on screen | Made to Stick (simple, concrete), Hormozi |
| 5 | **The viewer is the hero.** Their pain first ("ninte review…"), our fix second | StoryBrand, Cialdini (pre-suasion), audience research |
| 6 | **Open a loop early, pay it off inside the reel.** Never bait-and-switch | Hoyos/Kallaway, Hormozi (reward), skills honesty rules |
| 7 | **Something changes every 2–4 s.** In talking heads it's text/punch/insert; in builds the action itself counts | Our data (rule 5), MrBeast guide, short-form-video-script |
| 8 | **Real phone footage, real objects, real numbers.** Polish doesn't predict views | Our data (rule 4), talking-head skill |
| 9 | **Value before the ask. ONE ask only.** Value reels: spoken or caption ask, **no CTA card**. Lead reels (DROP, PIW): keyword CTA card | Cialdini (reciprocity), Hormozi, skills; big-page validation (top reels show no on-screen CTA) |
| 10 | **Give a reason to send it.** Useful, makes the sender look smart, or emotional. Ask "who sends this to whom?" | Contagious (STEPPS), Mosseri (sends per reach) |
| 11 | **One small human moment** (a real fail, a real reaction, a real story) makes it memorable | Storyworthy, Talk Like TED, our data (rule 8) |
| 12 | **Stay in the niche and stay true.** Every claim is real for Shawn/TPS. No fake stats, results or urgency | Our data (rule 7), every skill's ethics section, our positioning |
| 13 | **Show access or proof in the first 2 s, and an evidence card by 10–15 s.** Our workshop, a real build, a real price, a real screenshot. "What we built/saw" beats "what I think" | Big pages: Arsh (5.9M), Ankur, Tanay, Shaz |
| 14 | **Give long reels a visible spine:** a numbered-promise pill (1/5, 2/5…) changing every 8–15 s | Big pages: Ankur (3.4–3.7M) |
| 15 | **Brand every series the same way:** the same pill, progress bar, key-word colour and end card | Big pages: Tanay, Ankur |

## 2. Audience lens (who we're talking to)
**One viewer:** a KTU S7/S8 (or M.Tech) student in Kottayam/Ernakulam/Kollam, 20–23, CS/EC/EEE, scared of reviews and viva, with a limited budget and suspicious of project centres.
*Evidence: complaint sites from outside Kerala (2014–16) + news + inference. **Not yet verified with real KTU student comments.** Replace with real quotes once Reddit/Instagram comments are read.*
- **Their likely fears:** recycled projects ("same project circulating across branches"), centres that vanish after payment, **being unable to answer in the viva**, plagiarism checks, no placement story.
- **Our angle against each:** unique to your team · we stay till the viva · **we teach you, so you can explain it** · original work · a project you can talk about in interviews.
- **Tone:** peer, "machane/bro" energy, dry Kerala sarcasm, self-aware humour. Not preachy, not corporate.
- **Never:** moralise about buying projects (show the risk + the alternative instead) · name colleges, teachers or competitors · touch caste, religion, party politics or election content · sexist slang · raw Malayalam film clips or film audio (copyright, business accounts are most at risk). Re-enact in your own voice instead.
- **Language bank:** to be filled from real comments/Reddit (manual task, see `audience_india_kerala.md`).

## 3. The writing procedure (follow it in order, every reel)

### Step 1: Brief (2 minutes)
Fill these in, or don't write the reel:
- **Viewer + moment:** "S8 ECE student, Phase II just started"
- **One idea:** one sentence
- **Proof we have:** a real demo, real number or real story (if none, change the idea)
- **One ask:** PROJECT / INTERN / TOOL keyword, *or* send, *or* save
- **Send test:** "___ will send this to ___ because ___"

### Step 2: Pick the format (template Part E)
F1 build · F2 talking + text · F3 Drop · F4 single-shot fail · F5 not-as-simple · F6 story · F7 reply-to-comment.

### Step 3: Write 8 hooks, score them, keep the best 2
Write at least 8, using at least 4 different templates below. **Score each 0–2 on 6 dimensions (max 12):**
| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gap** | no question in the viewer's head | mild | they *need* to know |
| **Specificity** | vague | some detail | number / name / object |
| **Truth** | not paid off / not true | partly | fully paid off and true |
| **Fit** | >3 s to land | ~3 s | <2 s |
| **Voice** | sounds like an ad | ok | sounds like Shawn |
| **Pull** | could scroll | maybe | a stranger stays |
**A 0 on Truth or Pull = rejected whatever the total.** Ship the top 1. Test the 2nd as a Trial Reel variant (same body, new opening).

**15 hook templates (Manglish; fill the blanks with REAL facts only):**
1. Stakes: "Ee one mistake [X] kond [N] projects reject aayi."
2. Number: "[N] days-il [project], budget [₹N] mathram."
3. Identity: "Nee [branch] [sem] aanenkil, topic edukkunnathinu munp ith kaanu."
4. Contrarian: "[Common advice] aanu ninte project kollunnath."
5. Mistake: "[N] teams-ne guide cheythu; mostly ellarum [mistake] cheyyum."
6. Problem-first: [failing demo] + "ith ninte project aanenkil, nilkku."
7. Payoff-first: [working demo] + "ith [N] hours-il [how]."
8. Numbered: "Final year project-ile [N] mistakes. [N]-aamathe aanu ettavum risky."
9. Transformation: "[Bad] to [good] in [time]. Enthu maatti?"
10. Question: "Viva-yil [X] chodichaal answer undo?"
11. In-media-res: "Submission-nu [N] divasam munp project [failed]. Pinne…"
12. Curiosity: "Guide parayilla, pakshe viva-yil [thing] chodikkum."
13. Authority: "[N] projects kandappol oru pattern manassilaayi."
14. Timeliness (true only): "[Deadline]-nu munp ee [N] things tick cheyyanam."
15. Negative flip: "[Popular idea] old aanu. Pakaram ith try cheyyu."
+ from our data: **FAIL** (sarcastic line over a real fail) · **NOT-SIMPLE** ("Looks simple? Think again.") · **NAME** (a known tool/part as the hook: "ESP32", "SOLIDWORKS")
*Rewrite each template fresh in Shawn's voice. Templates go stale when copied word for word.*

### Step 4: Build the body on the beat structure (template Part C)
- **B2 promise (open loop):** "3 things, the last one is the one most teams miss"
- **B3 value:** max 3 points (max 2 if the reel is ≤30 s). Each point = one visual. Name the thing as you show it
- **Re-hook halfway** (reels >30 s): "pakshe ithaanu real problem…"
- **B4 payoff:** answers the hook exactly. Show it, don't just say it
- **B5 ask:** one sentence + TXT-CTA, about 2 s
- **B6:** end card, or a loop (the last line leads back into the first)

### Step 5: Choose length by format (our data + skills reconciled)
| Format | Length |
|---|---|
| F2, F5, F7 (talking / explainer) | **30–45 s to start**; up to 60–90 s for numbered lists *if every beat adds new value* |
| F3 Drop | 45–60 s |
| F1 build, F6 story | 45–90 s **only if the action/story keeps changing**, otherwise cut to 45 |
| F4 fail/moment | 8–30 s, looped |
*Big pages show talking reels of 43–169 s reaching millions, so length is not a ceiling. As a new page, start short, then lengthen only the formats whose watch-% in Insights stays high.*

### Step 6: Write the spoken lines for the mouth, not the eye
- Short sentences, one idea per sentence, ~2.5–3 words/s
- **Write the English meaning first (template D0)**, with simple acting notes per beat. Manglish lines are only a suggestion: Shawn delivers the English meaning in his own natural Manglish. AI-written Malayalam reads unnaturally, so never force Shawn to read it word for word.
- **Default = Read Mode** (template D4b): every line verbatim, ≤12 words, with a [direction cue], read from a teleprompter under the lens, one file per beat. *(Alternative for confident days: memorise the first and last lines and riff the middle.)*
- Mark the **[pause]** before every key line (an editor cut point)
- English for technical terms (ESP32, review, viva), Malayalam for the emotion and the glue

### Step 7: Fill the sheet (template Part D) and run the 2 gates
**Gate A: Script QA (100 points, ship at ≥90):**
| Area | Pts | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Truth & accuracy | 30 | Every number, rule, price, date and claim is real and current. Demo labelled as a demo. KTU rules checked |
| Voice | 20 | Sounds like Shawn, peer tone, no ad-speak, no preaching |
| Hook & structure | 20 | Hook score ≥9/12, beats B0–B6 present, loop paid off |
| Duration & clarity | 15 | Within format length, change every 2–4 s, readable on mute |
| Ask & caption | 15 | One ask; keyword matches ManyChat; caption line 1 = search phrase; ≤5 hashtags; send trigger present |
**Gate B: Ethics (must all be yes):** no "readymade / we do it for you" · no fake numbers, reviews or urgency · client shown only with written permission · no film clips/audio · no caste, religion, politics, colleges or competitors named.

### Step 8: After posting, learn
Log in `scripts/<post date>_<REEL NAME>_<topic>.md`: views, 3-s hold, watch %, **sends**, saves, keyword comments, follows. Every Sunday: what was different about the top reel? Keep that, change one thing.

## 4. Tactics from the trends (Nov 2026)
- **Trial Reels:** test hook #2 on non-followers before posting to your followers.
- **Meta AI translation** covers Hindi and Tamil/Telugu/Kannada/Marathi/Bengali, **not Malayalam yet**. For Phase 3 (national), English or Hindi versions can reach wider later.
- **GATE 2027:** exams 6–21 Feb, results 19 Mar. That's an M.Tech/GATE content window *(verify on gate2027.iitm.ac.in)*.
- **Business-account audio:** use Instagram's licensed library for business, or the synth SFX/original audio from our pipeline. Never trending copyrighted songs.
- **Evergreen formats beat dated trends** for our niche: POV, "3 mistakes", before/after, "things I wish I knew in S7". Only use a dated trend if it fits the niche in under a minute of thought.

## 6. Worked example (to show the method; facts in [brackets] must be made real before use)
**Brief:** S8 EEE/ECE student, Phase II starting · one idea: "a project you can't explain will fail the viva" · proof: Shawn's real experience guiding teams · ask: send to a teammate · send test: "student sends to project partner because it's about *their* viva".
**Format:** F2 (talking + kinetic text), 28 s.
**Hooks scored:**
| Hook | Gap | Spec | Truth | Fit | Voice | Pull | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Viva-yil 'ithu engane work aakunnu?' chodichaal answer undo?" | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | **12** ✅ |
| "Final year project-ile 3 mistakes" | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 8 |
| "Readymade project vaangiyaal enthu sambhavikkum?" | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | 10 (Trial Reel variant) |
**Beats:** B0 Shawn FR-CU, POS-HOOK, holding a breadboard project, TXT-HOOK "VIVA-YIL ITHU CHODICHAAL?" → B1 the spoken question → B2 "3 questions panel always chodikkum. Moonnamathe aanu ellarum fail aakunnath" → B3.1–3.3 one question each, TXT-KEY + CUT-PUNCH → B4 "Ninte project ninakku explain cheyyaan pattanam. Athaanu njangal padippikkunnath" → B5 "Ninte project partner-nu ithu send cheyyu" + TXT-CTA "SEND TO YOUR TEAMMATE" → B6 end card.
*(The viva questions must be the real, common ones from Shawn's experience.)*
