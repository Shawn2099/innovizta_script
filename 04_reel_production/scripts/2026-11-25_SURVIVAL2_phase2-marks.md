## D0. READ THIS FIRST: the whole reel in plain English
**What this reel is about (one sentence):** "Project Phase II is 150 marks, and they aren't all given at the end. 7 are for a diary, two reviews check your plan and early results, and the final panel asks each of you. So do these 3 things during this break."
**Why we're making it:** it's specific, surprising and useful (most students don't know the mark split). People save it and send it to their project group. It positions us as the people who know how KTU projects are actually marked.
**Your role:** the senior who read the syllabus so they don't have to. Clear and confident, a bit of "did you know this?".
**Length:** about 42 seconds. 6 short pieces. Record each 3 times (the first one 5 times).
