## D0. READ THIS FIRST: the whole reel in plain English
**What this reel is about (one sentence):** "You need 100 activity points to get your KTU degree. S8 is about to start, so this break is the time to check your count, sort your proof file and ask when the portal closes."
**Why we're making it:** it's a useful "survival" reel that every S7/S8 student needs. People save it and send it to friends, which grows the page. It also shows we know KTU inside out. The consult is mentioned only in the caption (value reel = no sales on screen).
**Your role:** the senior who's seen people panic about this in the last month. Friendly warning, a bit of "bro, seriously, check this". Not a teacher, not scary.
**Length:** about 35 seconds. 6 short pieces. Record each piece 3 times (the first one 5 times).

**Why we did topic = activity points:** Survival #2 (25 Nov) is already "Phase II prep", so #1 covers the other thing every final-year student worries about.

**About the Manglish lines below:** they're only a suggestion. **Read the English meaning, then say it in your own natural Manglish.**
