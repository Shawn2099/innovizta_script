## D0. READ THIS FIRST: the whole reel in plain English
**What this reel is about (one sentence):** "Phase II has started. Ask your coordinator when the first review is, then plan back from it: lock scope and order parts, build each block, join them and get first results, and make sure everyone can explain it."
**Why we're making it:** it posts the week Phase II starts. A plan they can copy is the most saveable thing we can give, and the first review checks exactly this (schedule, who does what, early results).
**Your role:** the senior who has watched 80 teams do this. Calm, practical, a bit "trust me on week 1".
**Length:** about 40 s. 6 pieces.

| # | Time | What you SAY (meaning in English) | How to ACT | What the viewer SEES |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | 0–3 s | "Phase II has started. Here's how to walk into your first review ready." | Lean in slightly, straight to the lens | Hook: **PHASE II: *WEEK BY WEEK*** |
| **B2** | 3–7 s | "First, ask your coordinator the review date. Say it's six weeks away: plan it like this." | Hold up the week sheet for 1 s | **ASK THE *REVIEW DATE* FIRST** |
| **B3a** | 7–13 s | "Week one: lock what your project will and won't do, and order the parts. Parts take time to arrive." | 1 finger | Pill **WEEK 1 · SCOPE + PARTS** |
| **B3b** | 13–19 s | "Weeks two and three: build each block on its own, sensor, power, code, and test each one." | 2 fingers, then hands apart like separate blocks | Pill **WEEK 2–3 · BUILD BLOCKS** |
| **B3c** | 19–26 s | "Weeks four and five: join them, get your first results and data points, and write it all in the diary." | Bring your hands together | Pill **WEEK 4–5 · FIRST RESULTS** |
| **B3d** | 26–32 s | "Week six: slides, and every member should be able to explain the whole project." | Point around, "all of you" | Pill **WEEK 6 · EVERYONE EXPLAINS** |
| **B4** | 32–36 s | "One working block beats a perfect slide." | Small smile, slower | **ONE *WORKING* BLOCK > PERFECT SLIDES** |
| **B5** | 36–39 s | "Send this to your project group." | Point at the lens | **SEND TO YOUR PROJECT GROUP** |
| End | 39–41 s | — | — | Logo · Learn It. Build It. Own It. · Want A Plan For YOUR Project? · Free 1-Hour Team Consult |

**Careful:** don't say a review date (each college sets it). "Six weeks" is an example ("say it's six weeks away"), not a KTU rule.
