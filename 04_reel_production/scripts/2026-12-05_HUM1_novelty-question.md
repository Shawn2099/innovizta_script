## D0. READ THIS FIRST (plain English)
**What this reel is about:** every final-year student knows the moment the panel asks "so what's the novelty?". No words, just your face. It's relatable, people tag their project partner, and it builds the page's personality between the useful reels.
**Your role:** the student at the viva, confident one second, frozen the next.
**Length:** ~10 s, looped (no end card).

| Time | What you DO (no talking) | What the viewer SEES |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Confident, relaxed, small nod like you just finished presenting | Text (whole reel): **POV: the panel asks** |
| 2–3 s | (the question lands) your smile freezes | Text adds: **"so what's the *novelty*?"** |
| 3–6 s | Slow blink. Eyes slide to your left (your "teammate"), then back to the lens | — |
| 6–9 s | Tiny nervous laugh, start to open your mouth, close it, adjust your collar | — |
| 9–10 s | Back to the frozen smile (this matches 2 s, so it loops) | — |

**Acting tip:** smaller is funnier. No big faces, no hands on the head. Think "please don't ask me a follow-up".
