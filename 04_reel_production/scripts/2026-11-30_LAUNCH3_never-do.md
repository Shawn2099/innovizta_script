## D0. READ THIS FIRST: the whole reel in plain English
**What this reel is about (one sentence):** "There are 3 things we will never do, even if you pay us: hand you a readymade project, give the same project to two teams, or disappear before your viva."
**Why we're making it:** students fear project centres (recycled projects, vanishing after payment, not being able to explain in the viva). This reel says what we stand for, in public. It's our trust reel, posted the day before Phase II starts.
**Your role:** calm, firm, a bit proud. Like a senior setting his own rules. Not angry at anyone, and **never naming anyone**.
**Length:** about 40 seconds. 6 short pieces. Record each 3 times (the first one 5 times).

**⚠ Shawn: these 3 promises must be things you'll really keep for every team.** If any of them isn't true, tell me and we change it (fact sheet F1–F3).
