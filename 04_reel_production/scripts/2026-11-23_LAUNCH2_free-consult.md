## D0. READ THIS FIRST: the whole reel in plain English
**What this reel is about (one sentence):** "People think a free consult is a sales call. Here is what actually happens: we fix your topic and features, tie them to your career, add a real industry problem, and plan the work and components."
**Why we're making it:** this month's ask is "book a free consult before Phase II". Students won't book a call when they don't know what happens on it.
**Length:** about 42 s + 2 s end card. 5 pieces, already shot.

**No spoken ask was recorded.** The ask is carried by the on-screen CTA in the last seconds of B5, the caption and the pinned comment. That's allowed: text is hand-written, nothing is auto-captioned.
**On-screen wording on the research-paper point is softer than the spoken line on purpose:** we never promise that a paper gets accepted (Honesty rule). "Stronger for a paper" is a claim we can stand behind; "accepted" is not ours to promise.
