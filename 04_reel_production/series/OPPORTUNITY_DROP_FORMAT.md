**Conclusion:** nobody is a **face** that picks internships for Kerala B.Tech students, explains each one in Manglish, and is seen to check that it's genuine. Your edge is trust and curation, not volume.

**Why the timing works:** KTU requires internships, and final-year students can do up to 6 months. Kerala has its own internship sources (IGNITE at Technopark/Infopark/Cyberpark, ICFOSS (stipend around ₹7,500/month on past calls), KSUM, ASAP Kerala, Internship Kerala), but students find out about them late, through posters.

**The "comment for link" mechanic is allowed if you do it properly.** In 2024 Instagram said that asking for a specific comment word *can* hurt reach when the aim is to "hack" engagement. It then clarified that ManyChat-style keyword-to-DM is fine when it's a real way of delivering something. To stay safe:
- Give the value **in the reel itself** (company, role, stipend, deadline on screen). The DM only delivers the link.
- Use **one keyword that never changes** (e.g. `INTERN`). It turns into a weekly habit and it's honest.
- Add a **send/share** CTA too. "Sends per reach" is one of the signals Instagram's head has named as most important, and "tag that friend who still hasn't found an internship" is a natural fit for this topic.

**What makes Reels perform (consistent across 2026 guides):**
- The first 1–3 s decide the reel. Start with movement and text on screen (many people watch muted).
- Keep 30–90 s. Aim for **45–60 s** for this format.
- For talking-head videos, cut or change the frame **every 3–5 s**.
- Watch time, saves and sends matter more than likes.

## 1A. Business goal: internship reels feed project-assistance leads

The internship drop is **bait for the right audience**, not the product. Everyone who comments `INTERN` is a KTU B.Tech/M.Tech student, which is exactly the person who will need a mini project, main project or M.Tech dissertation build. Every reel must quietly show "these people actually build things".

**Funnel**
```
Reel (free internship value + workshop in background)
  → comment INTERN → DM with links
    → soft line in DM: "Final year / mini project? Reply PROJECT"
      → PROJECT reply → ask: branch, semester, domain, deadline → WhatsApp/call
        → project assistance enquiry
```
Track: comments → PROJECT replies → WhatsApp chats → paid projects. That last number is what matters, not views.

**Rules so it doesn't feel like an ad**
- About 90% of each reel is internships, about 10% brand. There's one brand line only (5 s): "Njan ___, The Project Shop-il ninnu. Njangal KTU students-nte projects build cheyyan help cheyyunnu."
- Always shoot in the workshop **with real projects visible** (robot, PCB, 3D printer). The background does the selling.
- Every 4th week, do a **"Project Shop Internship"** drop where one of the 3 is *your own* internship/volunteer slot. This gets you free help, content and authority.
- Raise the project CTA in the KTU project seasons (S7 project phase / S8 main project / M.Tech dissertation phases; check dates against the current KTU scheme). Lower it during exam weeks.

## 2. The format: "Internship Drop"

**One reel a week, same day, same time, same structure, same keyword.**

- **Length:** 45–60 s
- **Content:** **3 internships** per reel (save the best one for last), plus 1 "bonus" in the DM only (a reward for commenting)
- **Mix each week:** 1 Kerala/onsite (Technopark/Infopark/KSUM/ICFOSS/startup) + 1 remote/national + 1 "wildcard" (research/IIT/NIT summer, hackathon-to-intern, high stipend)
- **Keyword:** `INTERN` (never change it)
- **Series name on screen:** "INTERNSHIP DROP #01", "#02"... A numbered series gets people coming back.

### Selection checklist (an internship must pass all of these)
- [ ] Deadline is **at least 5 days** after posting day
- [ ] You can open the **official** application page (company careers page, Internshala, Unstop, govt portal)
- [ ] **No fee** charged to the student. Paid "internships" that are really courses are always out.
- [ ] Open to B.Tech students (check year and branch eligibility)
- [ ] You can say in one line **why it's worth it** (stipend / brand / skill / PPO chance)

## 4. Shooting guide (one person, phone)
> **Superseded (6 Nov 2026):** for shooting and editing specs (camera, framing, posture, text styles, cuts, safe zones), **`REEL_SCRIPT_TEMPLATE.md` wins** (Drop = format **F3**). The notes below are kept for reference.

**Setup (do it once, mark it with tape so every week looks the same):**
- **Phone:** rear camera (better quality than the selfie camera), **4K 30fps** so you can crop/punch in during editing without losing quality. Vertical 9:16. Lock exposure and focus (long-press on your face).
- **Height:** lens at **eye level** or very slightly above. Never from below.
- **Distance:** about **1–1.2 m**, framed from mid-chest up. Leave space above your head. Your eyes should sit on the **upper third** line.
- **Position:** stand or sit slightly off-centre, angled about 15° to the camera, then turn your face straight into the lens.
- **Light:** window or softbox at **45°** to one side, at face height. Your back should face away from bright light. Turn off tube lights directly overhead (they cause eye shadows).
- **Audio:** wireless lav mic clipped about 20 cm below your chin. Phone mic only as a backup. Turn off the fan.
- **Background:** your Project Shop workspace (robots, PCBs, tools) 1.5–2 m behind you, so it reads as "tech person" straight away. Add a small logo or prop that stays the same every week.
- **Wardrobe:** the same solid-colour tee/hoodie every week (no fine stripes). It becomes your "uniform".

**Recording technique:**
- Record **section by section** (hook, #1, #2, #3, CTA). Do 2–3 takes of each. Say the **hook 5 times** with different energy and pick the best.
- Leave **1 s of silence** before and after each line (makes cutting easy).
- Look **into the lens**, not at the screen. Stick a small arrow next to the lens.
- Hand gestures help: hold up 1/2/3 fingers for each internship, point down for the CTA.
- Total shoot time once set up: **about 20 minutes**.

**Screen recordings (B-roll):** record your phone screen opening each listing page (scroll to stipend and deadline). These go in the top half of the split layout.

## 5. Editing guide

**Layout per section:**
- Hook and CTA: **you full-frame**, with a punch-in zoom (110–120%) on key words.
- Internship segments: **split screen**, listing screen-recording on top 55%, you on the bottom 45%. Or show you full-frame with the info card as an overlay.
- Change the frame (cut / zoom / layout switch) **every 3–5 s**.

**Info card (same design every week):**
```
[Company logo]  COMPANY NAME
Role: ______
💰 ₹____/month   ⏳ __ months
📍 Kochi / Remote   🗓 Apply by DD MMM
```
Brand colour: orange `#FF8A00` (same as build_reel.py).

**Tools:** `pipeline/build_reel.py` already does the 9:16 crop, punch-in zoom every ~3 s, whoosh SFX, pop-in hook text, progress bar, logo and end card. Use it for the base, with HOOK LINE 1 = `3 INTERNSHIPS THIS WEEK`, LINE 2 = `DROP #__`, end lines = `Comment "INTERN"`. Then add the 3 info cards and split screens in CapCut. **No auto-captions.** Malayalam/Manglish transcription is unreliable, so only hand-typed key text.

**Music:** low background beat at about -25 dB under the voice. Trending audio is optional.

**Cover/thumbnail:** your face + `INTERNSHIP DROP #__` + `₹__K stipend` in big text. Keep the same template every week so the grid looks like a series.

## 6. Comment → DM setup (once)

1. Switch the Instagram account to **Professional (Creator/Business)**.
2. Connect **ManyChat** (free tier works to start), or another tool that uses Meta's official API.
3. Trigger: comment contains `INTERN` on any reel. Add a **second comment trigger, `PROJECT`**, for the Project of the Week reels (DM: project's block diagram/components + "want us to help build it?" → same qualifying questions as the DM `PROJECT` flow below).
4. Public auto-reply (rotate 3–4 versions so it doesn't look robotic): "DM check cheyy 📩", "Sent! All the best 🚀"
5. DM message:
   ```
   Hey! Ithaa ee week-ile Internship Drop #__ 👇
   1) ___ – link – apply by __
   2) ___ – link – apply by __
   3) ___ – link – apply by __
   🎁 Bonus (DM only): ___ – link
   ⚠️ We never ask for money. Any "internship" that asks for a fee = skip it.
   Next drop: [Friday] [time]. Follow cheyy so you don't miss it.

   🛠 Mini project / main project / M.Tech project tension undo?
   The Project Shop KTU students-ne idea → working prototype vare help cheyyum.
   Reply "PROJECT" 👇
   ```
   Second automation: a DM reply containing `PROJECT` → "Super! Branch, semester, domain (IoT/robotics/ML/...) and deadline parayamo?" → hand over to WhatsApp/human.
6. Optional: a "Want this every week?" button that collects their email or WhatsApp. This builds your own list.

**Caption:** 2 lines with search phrases ("KTU internship 2026", "Technopark internship") + the keyword again + **max 5 hashtags** (Instagram's cap since Dec 2025): `#ktu #internship #keralaengineers #btech #technopark`
