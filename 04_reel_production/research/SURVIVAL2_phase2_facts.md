## 2. Safe action items for the reel ("finish this during the break")

Each rests on the cited fact numbers. Phrase as "your syllabus (CS/Civil/Mech text) says". These are all true consequences; none claims a date.

1. **Get your Phase I report into a state you can build on.** Phase II is literally "in light of the report prepared under Phase I", and the Phase I targets list literature survey, objectives, design/methodology, work plan. Fix gaps in Phase I now. (#15, #16)
2. **Write a Phase II work plan with dates, tasks per person, and what you will show at each review.** Phase II has 3 committee evaluations (2 interim + final), and Interim 1 scores "adherence to project schedule" and "task distribution among team members" (5 + 5 of 25). (#8, #9)
3. **Have something running to show in the first review: interim results, even rough.** Interim 1 gives 5 marks to "interim results" with the poor band "no interim results to show". Final development is expected by the end, but early results are scored from the first review. (#9, #17)
4. **Start (or tidy) the project diary/daily log and get the guide signing it weekly.** The syllabus gives 7 of the guide's 30 marks to it and says it "shall be signed after every day/week by the guide". Also in the rubric: "No project journal kept" is the poor band. (#12, #18)
5. **Decide each person's part and be able to explain all of the project.** Several rubric lines are marked "[Individual Assessment]" (involvement, presentation, communication) and the guide must ensure "involvement in all stages". Every member should be able to present their own part and answer questions. (#14, #10, #11)
6. **Order parts, boards, materials and fix the budget now.** The adherence rubric rewards material procurement and a budget idea carried over from Phase I; hardware lead times eat Phase II. (#18; the lead-time point is common sense, say it as advice, not as a rule)
7. **Plan what the final will test: results, conclusions, and a demo to your guide.** 10 of 40 final marks are "quality of results/conclusions/solutions" and the guide's 30 includes 5 for "completion of the project": you demonstrate it to your guide and the guide verifies results. (#11, #12)
8. **Start the report skeleton and reference list early; use a consistent template.** The report is its own 30 marks (a fifth of the total). Rubric looks for standard format, properly cited references, numbered figures/tables/equations, consistent formatting; "mostly unacknowledged content" scores 0-11 of 30. In English. (#19, #20, #21)
9. **(Optional) Check whether your college wants a paper/IPR/expo entry.** The syllabus lists a conference or journal paper "if possible", University project expos, and IPR "if applicable" as Phase II targets. They are encouraged, not mandatory. (#17)

Keep reel to 5-6 of these. Strongest for a short reel: 1, 2, 3, 4, 8, 5.
