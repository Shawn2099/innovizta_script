# SURVIVAL1 Activity points: fact sheet (researched 6 Nov 2026)

Method note: PDFs were downloaded and text-extracted, so quotes below are verbatim from the documents. The ktu.edu.in site itself was not reachable via search/fetch (the app.ktu.edu.in tutorial PDF failed a TLS check; not bypassed). The KTU 2019 and 2024 regulation texts were therefore read from college-hosted copies of KTU documents. Say "college-hosted copy of KTU document" on anything shown on screen. Nothing below is from memory.

## 3. VERIFY / unknown

- **VERIFY: "2023 admits = 2019 scheme".** Derived from R1 ("from 2019 onward") plus the 2024 regs ("from 2024-25"). No document found says "2023 admission, 2019 scheme" literally. Tried: KTU S4/S5/S6/S7 + "2023 Admission" + "2019 Scheme" (found only S5 Honours/Minor "2023 Admission" notices without a scheme label in the snippets). Confirm with the college or a KTU notice.
- **VERIFY: KTU's own official 2019 per-activity table.** The official tutorial https://app.ktu.edu.in/downloads/student_activitypoints_tutorial.pdf failed TLS verification (not bypassed). Caps in #12/#13 come from college docs and disagree (NSS 8/10 vs 60 vs 70 across sources). Do NOT put per-activity numbers on screen until someone opens the official KTU table in a browser. ktunotes.in has yet another table and is an unaffiliated student site.
- **VERIFY: is the final-semester result or degree actually withheld under 2019 for missing points?** 2019 regs say only "mandatory for the award of the Degree" (R6.23). R6.10 (degree eligibility) lists curriculum, credits, no disciplinary action. The explicit "results may be withheld" wording exists only in the 2024 handbook.
- **VERIFY: deadline / semester for upload under 2019.** None found. Only: portal window is time-limited (college doc) and the 2023 circular's 20 July 2023 for that cycle. No 2025/2026 circular found. Tell students to ask their Faculty Advisor for this year's portal date.
- **VERIFY: how a student checks points on the portal.** No official step-by-step obtained. Only found: points show in the "consolidated academic report" (ktustudents.in 2019 post, secondary) and the college enters them. Open the KTU student login and check the exact menu before scripting.
- **VERIFY: per-activity max for hackathons, NPTEL, internships, IEDC under 2019.** MOOC with certificate = 50 and internship (5+ full days) = 20 appear only in the MACE table. Hackathons are not clearly in the 2019-style table. Do not claim hackathon points for 2019.
- **VERIFY: PwD / readmitted / scheme-migration rules under 2019.** Not found (2024 has them: PwD 60; migration 5 per semester per group).
- **Anecdotal pain points: NOT FOUND.** Searched Reddit r/KtuKerala and the web for 2019-scheme complaints; nothing usable surfaced. Do not claim "students often miss X" unless the founders say it from their own experience.
- **2025-26 changes:** Only the 2024-scheme Activity Points Handbook (hosted Dec 2025) found. No change to the 2019-scheme rules found; not confirmed either way.
- Not checked: any KTU circular after 2023 that relaxed or extended the 2019 rule; the ktu.edu.in circular list directly.
