**What this means:** Instagram is your **trust and proof layer** (students check you there before they pay ₹25K). It is **not** your main lead source for the next 3 months. Leads will come from **campuses, communities and local ads.** Followers are a vanity number here. What counts is **final-year students in 3 districts who know your name.**

### Work backwards from the January sales target
```
Target: 6 paid teams in Jan (≈ ₹1.5L)
  ÷ 25% consult→paid            = 24 consults
  ÷ ~55% show-up rate           = ~45 leads (WhatsApp chats / DMs) in Dec–Jan
Where 45 leads come from:
  Campus clinics (6 colleges × ~5)   ≈ 30
  Click-to-WhatsApp ads (₹3–6K)      ≈ 8–20
  Instagram keyword DMs              ≈ 5–10
  Communities (TinkerHub/µLearn)     ≈ 3–5
```
*(Conversion, show-up and ad figures are rough third-party benchmarks. Replace them with your own numbers after December.)*

## 2. The campus engine (main lead source)
> **Update 6 Nov 2026:** clubs (IEDC/IEEE) won't partner with a project-assistance company. **Run the clinic yourselves** (online / office / makerspace) and reach students through individuals, free tools and paid student pages instead. See **VISIBILITY_AND_DIRECT_REACH.md**. Ambassadors and "spread from the first team" below still apply.

**One repeatable package: the "Final Year Project Clinic"**
- 45 minutes, free, on campus or Google Meet, hosted through the college's **IEDC / IEEE SB / department association**
- Content (from your notes): how to choose a project that fits your career, what panels look for, block diagram in 10 minutes (show your diagram tool), a reviews/viva survival kit, then live Q&A on teams' ideas
- Ending: "Free 1-hour consult for any team. Scan this / WhatsApp us." Collect a **sign-up sheet** (team name, branch, topic, WhatsApp)
- **Film it** → 3–4 reels + 1 YouTube video. One clinic also gives you a week of content.

**Campus ambassadors:** one student per college (IEDC or IEEE member). Pay **₹1,000–1,500 per paid team** they bring (your ₹1K referral, opened to non-clients). No retainer, so it costs nothing unless it works. Give them a WhatsApp link with a pre-filled message: "Hi, [ambassador name] paranju…" so you can track who sent each lead.

**The spread step:** when a team from a college pays, ask them (once they're happy, around week 2) to (1) introduce you to 2 other teams, and (2) let you film their demo later. Their classmates are your next clients.

## 5. Local paid ads (the ₹3K, used sharply)
- **Meta Click-to-WhatsApp ad** (opens WhatsApp chat directly). Targeting: **25 km radius around Kottayam, Ernakulam (Kakkanad/Kalamassery) and Karunagappally**, age 19–24, interests engineering/KTU.
- Creative: your best-performing reel (only boost what already works) + "Free 1-hour project consult for your team."
- Benchmarks are weak and vary a lot. One study gives about ₹38 per WhatsApp conversation across India, and edtech lead estimates run ₹200–450. **Expect ₹3K to buy roughly 8–30 conversations.** Since one paid team = ₹25K, if December's test gets even 1 sale, **raise the budget for January.**
- Run it **Dec 20 – Jan 31**, when main-project anxiety peaks.
