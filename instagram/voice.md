## My positions

Three to five things I believe that some of my audience does not.

1. You should be able to explain and defend every line of your project.
2. A project should connect to your career, not just pass the semester.
3. Teaching beats doing it for you. We build with you, you own your work.
4. Proof over promises. We do not invent results, numbers or testimonials.
5. Practical help should be concrete: a free PCB, an at-cost 3D print, a
   cleaner diagram, not vague "guidance".
