## Decisions already made
- Kerala first in Manglish, English later · Shawn on camera · batch shooting from scripts
- **No clubs (IEDC/IEEE) route.** Taboo. Reach students directly.
- Public positioning as a **maker brand** (kits, 3D printing, PCB, tools, mentoring). Project help = "mentoring, we build it *with* you". Never "readymade" or "we do your project".
- Honest proof only: past work with permission, in-house demo builds. No fake clients or reviews.
- No auto-captions. Max 5 hashtags. Original content only.
- Capacity 10–30 teams/month · budget ≤ ₹3K/month · price ₹15K min, ~₹25K average
- Instagram = trust layer. Leads come mainly from direct reach + search + ads.
