## New patterns not covered by the rules

1. **Device or screen in frame by 0-2 s.** 10/10 show a physical product or a live screen by about 2 s (phone thrust in, watch box, TV, news card, payment UI at about 1.5 s, loan-document table at about 1.4 s). This is stronger and more specific than R1 "a subject doing something".
2. **Price or amount as a big on-screen number.** "6000 AED" (1.0M), the scam sum on the news card (212K), and in-app "Rs 500" are used as stakes. Captions carry prices too.
3. **Spec callouts as the payoff beats in long reviews.** One- or two-word overlays ("LTPO PANEL", "MEDIATEK", "11.3mm", "32GB LPDDR5X", "Intel Core Ultra 7") mark the review beats in 140-170 s shazzam reels. Latin caps for product names, spoken Malayalam for everything else.
4. **Wardrobe and light changes as pace.** DfAJ (3.2M) has 57 cuts in 141 s, flash and B&W transitions, and host outfit changes (vest, blazer, tee). The edit's energy comes from these, not only from cut length.
5. **Comparison-title framing.** "X vs Y" in a single title line (DfCg, 2.2M) states a buying question up front. It works as a promise for the first few seconds even though it is a label.
