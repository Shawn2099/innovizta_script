## Transferable engineering-demo techniques (phone + tripod)

1. Top-down tripod insert with a visible timer or counter. C_QuDd shows a 18:22 timer (sheet frame 4) and DUbe uses a running "Goals Scored: 0" HUD; both make progress readable on mute. Phone on a tripod overhead, hand-written counter overlay.
2. Start the first second on physical motion. Dew5 opens on a ball pour and C7Fe on a drone in flight. Film the build, test or drop mid-action, then cut to the question within 1 s.
3. "This is not X, it's Y" hook over a prop held at the lectern or bench. C7Fe holds the drone at the lectern. A phone can do the same with a build on a stand, hand-written words only (per the no-auto-captions rule).
4. Single-take stakes monologue on location (DT6). One phone, both people in frame, zero edits, joke payoff inside 22 s.

Not observed in these stills: slow motion and scale comparisons (no slow-mo frames, no size-reference inserts). Not claimed as techniques here.
