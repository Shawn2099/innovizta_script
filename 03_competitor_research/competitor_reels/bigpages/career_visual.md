## New patterns (not in the rules)

1. **Numbered promise pill as a spine.** "5 things before you decide to marry" (DU), "5 money rules" (DazW), and a cost ladder (DfPZ). Each item gets its own card, about every 8-15 s. Completing the count works as a retention loop. Top two money and marriage reels (3.4M, 3.7M).
2. **Access proof in the first 2 s, stacked with the creator's reaction.** A named employer or famous person appears in the hook: "We are here at Intuit" (C8c), "What is your name?" from Jensen Huang (DZEj, stacked split-screen with Arsh's live reaction below), "Microsoft invited me to" (DY3). The two highest-viewed reels in the sample do this.
3. **Evidence cards, not claims.** A real number or screen replaces the assertion within about 10-15 s: a rate card (Ddat), a cost-per-page table (DfPZ), a LinkedIn "1 Lakh open jobs" screenshot (CrJIF), a news screenshot (DYr0).
4. **Fixed series signature.** Tanay: a yellow progress bar along the bottom and an "@tanay…pratap (exact spelling unclear at thumbnail size) FOLLOW AND SHARE FOR MORE" end card. Ankur: a fixed pill with "#n" markers. Single highlight word in yellow or purple (Cpx "Bhaukaal", CtzOsi purple box).
5. **Audience-answer hook.** The hook asks the viewer to answer ("Who has coding in his college?", "Think, 1000, 5000?") and the reel answers within about 3 s ("Yes." at about 2.3 s, CtzOsi).
