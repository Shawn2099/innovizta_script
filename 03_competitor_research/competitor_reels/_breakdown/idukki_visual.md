## PATTERNS

- Top-tier hooks show cause and effect in the first 1-3 s: a printer failing in the air (Dd8Hrd, 250k), a hand gesture that lights a bulb at about 1 s (C9mbh, 68.5k). Low-tier openings have no action at all: a talking head with a title (DfXIB, 6.2k) or drifting clouds (Deic3, 4.6k).
- Hook text, when used, is short, bold, and sarcastic or specific ("EXTRAORDINARY SKILLS TRYING TO PRINTING ON AIR"). A title-only label ("PROJECT BIKE SAFETY SYSTEM") did not perform.
- A face is not required: Dd8Hrd (250k) and DeLZf (61k) have none. Both show a physical object or body part doing something visible.
- A late reveal worked only with a story: C5GN9 (125k) has no hook and reveals at about 64 s, carried by a "my first project" angle. DfXIB (6k) has no reveal at all.
- Top reels run 57-90 s, with 0-1 cuts. The two lowest run 102 s (DfXIB) and 31 s (Deic3), so length alone does not explain tiers.
- Polish did not predict views. DfXIB (lowest-but-one) is a clean tripod shot. Top reels are simple handheld or tripod framing.
- Named products and search terms help: Dd1bA (21.5k) names a product ("Ola CAN tool") in its hook.
- Branding is not a differentiator: "IDUKKI VLOGZ" appears on almost every reel, top and low.
