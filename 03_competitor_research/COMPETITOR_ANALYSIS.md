### @idukki._vlogz._ (the leader, 96K)
**What he posts:** almost daily build reels from his workshop (bike safety system, Raspberry Pi oscilloscope, spider robot with thermal vision, landslide detection with GSM, hand-therapy glove, robotic arm), plus 3D-printing, repair and some personal posts.

**The baseline (7–15K)** is "project name + build + demo". The cover always names the project in big text ("PROJECT BIKE SAFETY SYSTEM").

**His formula:** *a real face × a real workshop × a project students recognise × daily posting*, plus occasional humour or personal posts that bring new followers.
**Weaknesses:** comments are low on normal posts, there's no lead capture (just a YouTube link), nothing about internships or careers, and nothing KTU-specific.

### @febtech.ai (direct project-assistance rival, 4.7K)
**What he posts:** 1–3 reels a day, **faceless**, with a bold title on the cover ("Is Solar Reliable Over DG", "Decoding Com Signals", "Feedback Control using Laser Based Camera Vision"). Topics jump between solar EPC jobs, electrical panels, an RC speedboat, PCBs, X-rays and an AI tool.

## 3. What actually reaches KTU students

From the reel data, the winners share these 7 things:

1. **A real face in a real workshop builds trust.** The top account (96K) does it, and the faceless rival has no personality to follow. But a face alone doesn't bring comments: Idukki also gets only 2–10 on normal reels. **Comments come from the topic and the prompt** (Flemin's career and struggle posts get 20–128, and your keyword CTA gives people a reason to comment).
2. **High posting frequency.** Idukki posts about 6 a week and Febtech about 10. Posting once a week with this audience won't build momentum. **Minimum 4 a week. The Internship Drop is 1 of them, not the whole plan.**
3. **A project name students recognise, in big cover text.** "Bike Safety System", "Gesture Control with OpenCV", "Landslide Detection with GSM". These are project titles students search for and picture themselves building.
4. **Question or POV hooks.** "Can a…", "What if…", "Looks simple? Think again", "POV: you joined an internship thinking…".
5. **Career, internship and student-success stories go viral** (Flemin's top 4 reels; bbx's 79.9K fear hook).
6. **Humour, failure and vulnerability bring in followers** (Idukki's 250K fail, 60.9K health post, Flemin's 128-comment struggle story). The regular build reels keep followers. The human ones grow the page.
7. **Malayalam-first emotion:** Kerala pride ("Engineering from Kerala"), Manglish banter in the comments, Malayalam text on screen.

**What nobody is doing (your gap):**
- ~~Nobody uses a comment-keyword funnel.~~ **Corrected 6 Nov:** Flemin uses one ("Type CYBERSECURITY below", 57.7K). The keyword mechanic is *proven*, not unique. The gap is that **nobody runs it as a recurring, numbered, KTU-specific series** tied to project help.
- Nobody runs a **recurring, numbered KTU series**.
- Nobody is a **face for KTU project + internship help** (Idukki = maker, Febtech = faceless engineering, Flemin = his own company).

## 4. Your plan: 4–5 reels a week (recommended), 2 a week minimum

Competitors post 2–10 times a week. **Recommended: 4–5 a week, batch-shot.** If that's too much for one host, **minimum 2 a week:** the Friday Internship Drop plus one alternating Project of the Week / Human reel. Start with 2 and add slots as the shooting routine gets faster.

| Day | Series | Format | Length | Cover | CTA (keyword) | Copies what works for… |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Project of the Week** | Face intro → build B-roll → working demo → "KTU mini/main project-nu perfect" | 30–45 s | **PROJECT NAME** in big text | Comment `PROJECT` → DM with block diagram/components + "want us to help build it?" | Idukki baseline + Febtech covers |
| Tue | **Question / myth** | "Can a ₹500 sensor detect a landslide?" Answer with a demo | 20–30 s | The question | Share / save | Febtech's question hooks |
| Wed | **Human / relatable** | Fail of the week, POV student/viva humour, behind the scenes, founder struggle | 15–30 s | Funny or POV line | none (to grow reach) | Idukki 250K, Flemin 33.6K |
| Fri | **Internship Drop #__** | As in OPPORTUNITY_DROP_FORMAT.md | 45–60 s | DROP #__ + ₹ stipend | `INTERN` → DM → PROJECT upsell | Flemin's career reels |
| Sat (alternate weeks) | **Student win / campus** | Student you helped demos their project, or a college visit (IEDC/IEEE SB) | 30–45 s | "She built this in 3 weeks" | `PROJECT` | Flemin's ZUKI and CET visit |

**Batch shoot:** shoot all 4–5 in **one 2–3 hour session a week** with the same setup (see the format doc, section 4). The workshop setup gets reused.

**Pin 3 reels:** (1) your best project reel, (2) the latest Internship Drop, (3) "Who we are + a student project we built". Idukki pins evergreen #finalyearproject reels, and they're his 2nd and 3rd most viewed.

**Hashtag pool (seen on the top reels). Use max 5 per post, Instagram's cap since Dec 2025:** `#keralastudents #finalyearproject #ktu #btech #malayalam #engineeringstudents #arduinoproject #iotproject #kerala`

### Hook bank, adapted from the winners
- "Ee project vechu viva-yil teacher-ne njettikkam." (Project of the Week)
- "₹___ components, 3 days. Ithaa working [project]." 
- "Looks simple? Think again." *(question pattern)*
- "POV: Main project submission naale, circuit work aavunnilla 😭" *(relatable)*
- "POV: Internship cheyyunnathu certificate-nu vendi maathram aanennu vicharichu…" *(career POV)*

## 5. Getting distribution (shortcuts)

1. **Collab posts** (Instagram's Collab feature, where the reel appears on both pages): offer @ktunotes.in or @ktumagic the Internship Drop as a co-post. It's useful content for their audience, so it's an easy yes.
2. **Paid shoutout:** @ktu_updates (35K) openly says "DM for Promotions". Test one shoutout of your best Internship Drop and measure INTERN comments per ₹.
3. **College tie-ups:** IEDC, IEEE student branches, tech fests. Film the visit (Flemin's CET reel = 9.5K + trust).
4. **Comment in Malayalam on the big pages' posts** (ktumagic, ktunotes, idukki) as The Project Shop, helpfully and without spam. Their comment sections are your audience.
5. **Reply to every comment in the first hour.** Your rivals average 2 comments a reel, so being visibly responsive stands out.
