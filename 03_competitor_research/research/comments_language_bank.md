## What it tells us for scripts
1. **Tech questions in the comments = free content.** Answer them with **F7 (reply to comment)**: "is that NodeMCU or ESP32?" is a ready-made hook.
2. **Address viewers as "bro / machane"**, and use "mone" for warmth. Hype words: "adichu keri", "poli", "kidu" *(the last two are common Kerala slang, not seen in this sample; check on our own page)*.
3. **People bond with the person, not the brand** (the get-well comments). Shawn's real moments matter (STORY/HUM formats).
4. **Keyword CTAs hide the comment section.** Flemin and bbx both show "comments limited / no comments yet" on their keyword reels. Ours should keep comments open and reply publicly too (ManyChat public replies), so the reel still looks alive.
5. **Expect pushback on hype claims** ("FDE ella companylum ila"). Keep claims precise and true.
