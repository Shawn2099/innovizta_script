# Books and frameworks for 30-60 s face-cam Reels (Kerala engineering-project mentoring)
Researched 2026-11-06. Paraphrased. Most web sources are secondary summaries (search found no primary or official pages for several items); confidence is flagged per item. No burned-in speech captions (house rule); only short hand-written hook text.

## 1. Hook Point (Brendan Kane)
Sources: https://llm-book-kb.vercel.app/kb/authors/brendan-kane , https://summaries.com/blog/hook-point (secondary; the book's step-by-step process was NOT verified)
- Core: a "hook point" is a deliberate element that earns attention in about 3 seconds and can hold it afterwards. Be specific to something the viewer cares about, and test many variants.
- Reel: write 10+ first-line variants per idea, post the best, reuse the winning pattern.
- Example: "Final-year project submit cheyyan 3 weeks maathram - ithu cheythillel fail aakum" beats "Project tips".

## 2. Contagious (Jonah Berger, STEPPS)
Sources: https://www.supersummary.com/contagious-jonah-berger/summary/ , https://lifeclub.org/books/contagious-jonah-berger-review-summary
- Core: ideas spread when they carry Social Currency (sharer looks smart), Triggers (cued by everyday context), Emotion (high-arousal), Public visibility, Practical Value, Stories.
- Reel: make something a student would send to a teammate; end on a share-worthy line.
- Example: "KTU mini-project topics that look advanced but take 2 weeks" (practical value + social currency); trigger: the semester-end record-book panic everyone knows.

## 3. Made to Stick (Heath brothers, SUCCESs)
Source: https://summaries.muthu.co/posts/influence_and_motivation/contagious (comparison only; the acronym Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories is from general knowledge, not verified this session)
- Core: one simple core message; break an expectation then open a curiosity gap; concrete images over abstractions; credibility via proof.
- Reel: one message per Reel; show the real circuit, PCB or app on screen.
- Example: hold the working demo and say "Viva-il 'ithu enthinu?' ennu chodhichaal 1 sentence-il parayanam".

## 4. Influence / Pre-Suasion (Cialdini)
Sources: https://justinowings.com/presuasion-cialdini/ , https://nextbigideaclub.com/magazine/conversation-mastering-the-art-of-pre-suasion-with-robert-cialdini/20494/ , https://pracademy.co.uk/insights/review-pre-suasion-and-the-persuasion-code/
- Core: reciprocity, liking, social proof, authority, scarcity, consistency, plus Unity (shared identity). Pre-suasion: what attention is directed to just before the ask shapes the response; ethical only when highlighting true things.
- Reel: give value first; show real student results; speak as "nammude Kerala engineering students"; scarcity only if genuine batch limits; dwell on the pain before the CTA.
- Example: spend 40 s fixing a real error, then "ithu poleyulla help venamenkil DM 'PROJECT'".

## 5. $100M Leads (Alex Hormozi) - hook / retain / reward
Sources: https://margin-notes-600.pages.dev/frameworks/three-retention-methods , https://margin-notes-site.pages.dev/p/framework/content-unit-hook-retain-reward.html (third-party and low quality; directional only)
- Core: every content unit = Hook (curiosity and relevance), Retain (lists, steps, stories), Reward (payoff proportional to time spent); one unit's reward can set up the next hook.
- Reel: promise in line 1, deliver in steps, payoff near the end, tease the next Reel.
- Example: "3 mistakes that get projects rejected at review" -> three numbered mistakes -> fix checklist -> "part 2: viva questions".

## 6. Building a StoryBrand (Donald Miller)  [not web-verified this session; general knowledge]
- Core: the customer is the hero, you are the guide (empathy plus authority); problem, plan, call to action, stakes of success vs failure.
- Reel: open on the student's problem, not your credentials; give a 3-step plan; one CTA.
- Example: "Guide help cheyyunnillenkil? Step 1 topic, step 2 base paper, step 3 demo - DM 'PLAN'."

## 7. Storyworthy (Matthew Dicks)
Sources: https://www.artofmanliness.com/articles/how-to-tell-better-stories/ , https://lifeclub.org/books/storyworthy-matthew-dicks-review-summary
- Core: a story is built around a roughly 5-second moment of change; begin at the opposite state; personal stakes.
- Reel: 30 s micro-story: before, the moment, after.
- Example: "Demo-kku 10 min munpu code run aakunnilla... oru wire loose aayirunnu" -> lesson.

## 8. Talk Like TED (Carmine Gallo)  [not web-verified; general knowledge]
- Core: emotional connection, a novel or jaw-dropping moment, memorable structure, rule of three, conversational delivery.
- Reel: one surprising demo or fact, three points maximum, talk to one friend.
- Example: show the project working in the first 3 s, then "ithu 3 steps-il cheythu".

## 9. MrBeast production guide (leaked 2024; authenticity and details vary by report)
Sources: https://passionfru.it/mrbeast-leaked-document-81151/ , https://www.creatorhandbook.net/leaked-document-allegedly-reveals-mrbeasts-secrets-to-youtube-success-the-key-takeaways/ , https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/memo-how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/
- Core: the first minute is the most critical; deliver what the title and thumbnail promised; study retention graphs and fix dips; re-engage at regular points.
- Reel: first 2 s must match the hook text; cut every pause; re-hook mid-way ("but here is the catch").
- Example: hook text "Project reject aayi?" with an immediate spoken line about the rejection; midpoint "ippo main thettu varunnu".

## 10. Paddy Galloway (YouTube strategy)
Sources: https://www.colinandsamir.com/resources/the-new-rules-of-youtube-from-paddy-galloway , https://podcast.creatorscience.com/paddy-galloway/ (secondary)
- Core: packaging (title plus thumbnail) explains much of the gap between average and huge; set explicit idea criteria; generate many variants; do not over-react to micro retention dips.
- Reel: treat cover frame, hook text and first spoken line as one package; draft 30 hooks, keep the best.
- Example: 30 variants of "Final-year project choose cheyyumbol ee 5 thettukal".

## 11. Short-form hook formulas (Kallaway, Jenny Hoyos)
Sources: https://briefread.pages.dev/en/summary/how-to-create-irresistible-hooks-and , https://vidiq.com/blog/post/how-jenny-hoyos-gets-10m-views-per-youtube-short/ , https://www.contentgrip.com/how-to-tell-a-great-story-in-60-seconds/ , https://marketing-examined.beehiiv.com/p/jenny-hoyos-shortform-video-playbook (secondary recaps, not original transcripts)
- Kallaway 3-step hook: context, scroll-stop interjection, contrarian snapback; short staccato sentences.
- Hoyos: hook (about 3 s), foreshadow (what is coming, the conflict, what you will get; list the steps), a breathing transition, then payoff.
- Example: "Final-year project cheyyunnavar... STOP... pakshe topic choose cheyyunnathu thettaanu."

## 12. Instagram / YouTube guidance
Sources: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-tips-for-reels-creators/721054/ (reports Instagram's own tips incl. grab attention in first 3 s); https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/perfect-youtube-short-length-structure-hooks (third-party; no official YouTube Help page found)
- Core: hook in the first ~3 s; Instagram insights expose skip rate; design for sound-off viewing; benchmark percentages (e.g. "80% watch muted") are vendor claims.
- Reel: 5-8 word hand-written hook text at 0 s; test the first 3 s muted.
- Example: on-screen "Project fail aakaathirikkan" with direct eye contact.

## Talking-to-camera delivery (synthesis, no single book)
Eyes on the lens; start mid-thought (no "hi guys"); one idea per breath; short sentences; energy one notch above normal; cut gaps; finish on the payoff, then the CTA.

## 10 laws of a script that works
1. Open with a specific problem or promise to a named viewer in the first 3 s - Kane, Instagram guidance, MrBeast guide, Kallaway.
2. Hook text, spoken line and visual must say the same thing - MrBeast guide, Hoyos.
3. Write many hooks, ship the best - Kane, Galloway.
4. One core idea per Reel, concrete and visible - Heath brothers, Hormozi.
5. Open a curiosity loop and pay it off inside the Reel - Hoyos, Kallaway, Hormozi.
6. Give value before the ask - Cialdini, Hormozi.
7. Viewer is the hero; show the pain before the fix - StoryBrand, Cialdini (pre-suasion).
8. Give a reason to share (useful, makes sender look smart, emotional) - Berger.
9. Use one tiny moment of change for emotion - Dicks, Gallo.
10. Re-hook mid-way, cut dead air, end on payoff plus one CTA - MrBeast guide, Hoyos, Hormozi.
