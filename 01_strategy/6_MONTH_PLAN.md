## 2. The flywheel: every task feeds the others

```
        DEMO BUILD / CLIENT PROJECT / FREE TOOL
                 (one piece of real work)
                          │
   ┌──────────────┬───────┴──────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
   ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼
 Reel(s)      YouTube video   Website page   Google profile   Kit / tool
 (trust)      (search)        (search)       photos (local)   (product)
   └──────────────┴───────┬──────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
                          ▼
            Students FIND us (Google / IG / YouTube)
            or are REACHED (ambassadors, shoutouts, ads, Clinic Live)
                          ▼
              Free 1-hr consult → paid team
                          ▼
     Review + proof clip + referral + ambassador at that college
                          └──────────► feeds the next round
```
**Rule:** never make something that only feeds one channel. Every build, clinic, tool and client should produce at least 3 assets.

## 3. Rules that stop work from conflicting
1. **One message everywhere:** *"Engineering projects: learn it, build it, own it."* Project help = **mentoring, built with you.** Never "readymade" or "we do your project" (that would clash with the trust positioning, Meta's ad rules and the club taboo).
2. **One main call to action per month** (table below). Every reel, ad, shoutout and DM pushes the same thing that month.
3. **Promote free things in public** (Clinic Live, free tool, guides) and **sell in DMs/consults.** Paid shoutouts and ads never say "project help".
4. **Honest proof only:** demo builds are called demos, client work is shown only with permission.
5. **Workload cap for Shawn:** about 15 h/week on marketing. If something has to give, reels drop from 4 to 3 before consults or follow-ups slip.
6. **Capacity guard:** at 25 active teams, say "next slots open on __" (true scarcity only).
7. **Budget:** ₹3K/month from savings. **Anything extra only comes out of revenue**, and only into a channel that has already produced a paid team.
8. **No dead channels:** old students, IEDC/IEEE and other club routes are dropped.

## 6. Month by month

### November: Foundations + pre-sell S8 ("be findable")
**Why:** S8 teams are **on break and about to start Phase II**. Every later reel, ad and shoutout sends people to your profile, Google listing and website, so they need to be credible first. Meanwhile, start talking to S8 teams before Phase II begins.
- **Visibility (#6, #2):** website v1 (home, offer, FAQ, WhatsApp, consult form, no fake counters) · Google Business Profile + **5 reviews from this year's clients** · Instagram with search-optimised Name/bio · directories · Search Console
- **Brand (#1):** positioning line, bio, wording rules, cover template
- **Content (#3):** 3 launch story reels + "Phase II starts in December: 5 things to finish this break" + Survival + Opportunity Drop · **Demo Build #1** · **bank 6 reels** for December
- **Funnel (#8):** ManyChat, WhatsApp labels, lead tracker, consult script
- **Reach (#7):** 3 ambassadors/creators via the 5 clients · ₹1K shoutout test pointing to the free "Phase II planning" Clinic Live (end of Nov)
- **Exit check:** website + Google profile + Instagram live · 5 reviews · ManyChat working · 10+ reels · 6 consults

### December: Convert #1 (Phase II starts)
**Why:** S8 classes and Phase II start around 1 Dec. Teams need hardware, a plan and a working prototype before Review 1.
- **Big rock:** fill the consult calendar (15 → 4 paid). Reply within 1 hour, follow up within 24 h
- **Content:** Project Idea of the Week ×2/week, "Plan Phase II in 6 weeks", "what Review 1 panels check", diagram-tool teaser · **referral campaign** (free internship + ₹1K off per team)
- **Reach:** **Click-to-WhatsApp ads all month** (mentoring wording, 25 km, 18–24) · Clinic Live #2 · free tool #1 launched ("50 project ideas" / diagram lite)
- **Visibility:** city pages + YouTube #1–2
- **Exit check:** 4 paid · 8 reviews · cost per consult known for each channel

### January: Convert #2 (Review 1, ~18–23 Jan)
**Why:** teams that slipped in December panic before Review 1. Also M.Tech S4 zeroth evaluation (~13 Jan).
- **Big rock:** "Review 1 in 2 weeks?" consults (honest about what's achievable) + delivery for December teams
- **Content:** "Review 1 survival kit", "circuit not working: 5 checks", M.Tech dissertation tips · handover checklist running for every team
- **Reach:** ads on the Review 1 angle · ambassadors push the free tool in class groups
- **Exit check:** 4 paid · 12 reviews · first 3 proof pieces

### February: Deliver + tool beta (Review 2, late Feb)
- **Big rock:** **student tool beta** (diagram/report automation, free tier) + TOOL funnel · ambassadors up to 6
- **Content:** build-in-public on the tool, permissioned client behind-the-scenes, Review 2 prep · Opportunity Drops = summer internships
- **Exit check:** 2 paid · 100 tool users · 5 proof pieces

### March: Finish strong (final evaluation 17–26 Mar)
- **Big rock:** report/PPT/plagiarism-safe writing content + **report and diagram consults** (your diagram/data tooling shines here) + the **proof bank**
- **Content:** report structure, PPT before/after, graphs from data · M.Tech interim evaluation (~25 Mar) tips
- **Exit check:** 2 paid · 18 reviews · 8+ proof pieces

### April: Harvest + next season (viva ~20 Apr)
- **Big rock:** **July waitlist**: current S6 students become S7 in July and start Project Phase I. "Pick your project early: free planning session in June." Plus **M.Tech S4 thesis** (upload ~22 May)
- **Content:** viva quick-fire, client final demos (permissioned), "what __ teams taught us" (true numbers)
- **Reach:** ambassadors up to 10 · waitlist ad
- **Review:** cost per paid team by channel → plan May–Oct · English-track decision
- **Exit check:** 25+ waitlist · 20+ reviews · 10 ambassadors

## 8. Risks to watch
- The KTU even-semester 2026-27 calendar isn't published yet → **re-check in December** and move review dates if needed
- January demand is lower than expected → lean harder on ads + Clinic Live, and push the referral offer
- An ad rejected or an account flagged → fix the wording, don't try to get round the review
- Burnout: keep the 15 h/week cap and batch days
- Targets are estimates from thin benchmarks → **recalibrate at every monthly exit check**
