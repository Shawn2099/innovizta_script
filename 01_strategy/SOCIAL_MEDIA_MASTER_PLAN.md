## 1. How the algorithms work in 2026 (what we design for)

### Instagram
| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Reels are ranked mainly on **watch time, likes per reach, and sends (shares) per reach**. Likes matter most for followers, **sends matter most for reaching non-followers**. | Mosseri, Jan 2025 (reported) |
| **Originality:** accounts that mostly repost content get dropped from recommendations. Adding a watermark, border or credit doesn't count as original. | Instagram @creators, **official**, 30 Apr 2026 |
| **Max 5 hashtags** per post/Reel. Hashtags barely help reach. Keywords in the caption, on-screen text and speech do the work. | Dec 2025, widely reported |
| Public posts from professional accounts are **indexed by Google**, so captions should read like search phrases ("KTU mini project ideas ECE"). | Jul 2025 (reported) |
| **"Your Algorithm":** users pick topics, so **stay on one clear topic** (engineering projects/careers). Off-topic posts (music, random vlogs) confuse who you're shown to. Flemin's music reels got 451–2K views vs 10–38K for his career ones. | Dec 2025–Jun 2026 |
| **Trial Reels:** shown to non-followers first. Use them to test 2 hooks for the same reel. | 2025–26, schedulable |
| Comment-keyword → DM (ManyChat) is fine when it actually delivers something. Bare "comment 🔥" bait is not. | Instagram clarification, Jun 2024 |

**What that means for every reel:** hook in 1–2 s → keep people watching → end with a reason to **send it to a batchmate** ("send this to your project partner").

### YouTube
- Long-form recommendations rely on **click-through × watch time × viewer satisfaction**. A new channel has no audience, so **search is its engine.** Title = what a student types ("IoT final year project ideas ECE KTU 2027").
- Shorts can be up to 3 min (official). **Link each Short to a long-form video** (Studio "Related video").
- Upload **clean files with no Instagram watermark** as Shorts.
- **Test & Compare:** up to 3 thumbnails per video, and it picks the winner by watch time.
- *(These YouTube points come from third-party reports. Confirm them in YouTube Studio as you go.)*

**Gap:** no active Malayalam channel covers KTU projects, Arduino/ESP32/IoT, project ideas or M.Tech/GATE (Haiku channel scan). YouTube in Malayalam is wide open.

## 3. Content system: 4 Instagram reels a week + 1 YouTube video every 2 weeks

Each reel slot is a named series that repeats every week. Recurring series train both the algorithm and your audience.

| # | Series | What | Length | Keyword funnel | Based on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opportunity Drop #__** | Internships in odd weeks, certifications in even weeks. 3 picks, checked. | 45–60 s | `INTERN` → DM links → upsell | Flemin's top reels = career topics |
| 2 | **Project Idea of the Week** | *"KTU final year project idea: ___. Ithaa njangal build cheythu nokki."* An **in-house demo build** (or a past client project, with permission, or an Innovizta build) → demo → "who it suits, rough difficulty, what you'd learn". Builds become sellable kits. | 30–45 s | `PROJECT` → DM block diagram + components + free Google Meet consult | Idukki's 7–15K baseline |
| 3 | **B.Tech / M.Tech Survival** | From your notes: activity points, CV, LinkedIn, which club, seminar paper, LaTeX, plagiarism, paid vs free internship, worthwhile certifications, what recruiters look for | 20–40 s | Save/send CTA (optional `GUIDE` → PDF checklist) | Nobody covers activity points |
| 4 | **AI for your project / Build in public** | AI tools that save project or report time (honest, ethics-safe), **plus your own student tools and internal tools as they ship** | 20–40 s | `TOOL` → early access list | Turns tool-building into content |
| + | **Human** (1–2 a month) | Fail of the week, POV viva/review humour, M.Tech life, founder struggle. Collab with Clueless Engineer. | 15–30 s | none (reach) | Idukki 250K fail, Flemin 128-comment story |

**Stories, daily:** poll/quiz ("Which project next?"), deadline reminders for the Opportunity Drop, reposts of student DMs/screenshots (with permission).
**1 carousel a week:** turn the Survival reel into slides ("Activity points: full guide"). Carousels get saved.

### Launch: first 3 weeks (replaces the "intro reels until we've explained everything" idea)
Keep intros to **3 story reels**, mixed in with value reels from day 1, then pin them:
1. **"Why I started The Project Shop"**: Shawn's B.Tech struggle → M.Tech → "nobody helped us, so we help you". Tell it as a story, not a services list.
2. **"What happens in your free 1-hour consultation"**: walk through it, show the room and the 3D printer. This is your strongest offer, so show it.
3. **"What we will never do"**: no readymade projects, you own your work. Builds trust and answers objections.

Pinned reels: #1, the latest Opportunity Drop, and #2.

### YouTube (from week 3)
- **1 long-form video every 2 weeks**, 8–20 min, Manglish, **16:9**, chapters, a search-phrase title. Shoot it on the same day as the reels.
- Starter topics (search-first):
  1. "How to choose your KTU final year project (ECE/EEE/CS)"
  2. A full build of a Project of the Week, parts → demo
  3. "Activity points: complete guide"

  5. "IEEE paper → seminar → LaTeX, step by step"
  6. "Free 1-hour consultation: real session" *(only with the team's permission)*
- Shorts: post the reels as clean files, each linked to the related long-form video.

## 3A. Showing work when there are no active clients (honest version of "fake it till you make it")
Fine (it's real): **past projects** from last season's 80 teams (with permission, names blurred), **demo builds you make in-house** ("we built this to show what's possible"), **Innovizta builds**, your **tools** (diagram automation), the **workshop and process**, and the real number **"80 teams last season"**.
Not fine: implying they're current clients, "our student built this" when they didn't, made-up numbers, reviews or screenshots. One student calling it out in the comments costs more trust than the reel ever earns, and trust is your whole moat.
**Upside:** demo builds become **Project Idea of the Week** content, **YouTube full-build videos**, and **kits you can sell**. One build gives you three assets.

## 3B. The offer (say it the same way everywhere)
- **Free 1-hour Google Meet consultation** for the whole team (idea → career-linked project plan)
- Project from **₹15K, typically ~₹25K**. Includes circuit diagram, data points/tables, report support material and **a class teaching the team their project**
- **Internship on your own project** (small fee). **Refer a team and it's free, plus ₹1,000 off for every team you refer**
- Branches: **CS, EC, EEE, robotics**: software, IoT, hardware + simulation (no civil or heavy mechanical)

**Unit economics:** one ₹25K project pays for about 8 months of the ₹3K budget. Once a channel brings even one paid team, scale it (see section 6).

## 3C. Fast start: customers in the next 30 days (Instagram takes 2–3 months to build)
1. **Reactivate the 80 teams from last season.** That's where your word of mouth came from, and it stopped because those students left. WhatsApp each one personally:
   - ask for (a) permission to show their project, (b) a 10-second clip or photo if they still have it, (c) **referrals to juniors** (same ₹1,000 per team, paid to them)
   - each alum knows the S7/S8 batch below them
2. **Broadcast/Status on WhatsApp Business:** "Free 1-hour project consultation for S7/S8 teams this month."
3. **College outreach:** IEDC / IEEE student branch / department associations: offer a free 45-minute "How to pick and survive your final year project" session (on Google Meet or on campus). Film it for content.
4. **Timing:** S7/S8 teams are locking topics and reviews now, so **the consultation offer is the most urgent message in November–December.**

## 4. Funnel: views → free consultation → paid project

```
Reel / Short / YouTube
  → comment keyword (INTERN / PROJECT / TOOL)        [ManyChat, official API]
    → DM delivers the thing + "Free 1-hr consultation for your team?"
      → WhatsApp Business (quick replies + labels: New / Consult booked / Paid / Done)
        → 1-hour free consultation (book via a simple form or WhatsApp)
          → paid project (build WITH them) → internship on their project
            → ask permission for a proof screenshot / quote → content
```
- **Capacity 10–30 teams a month:** use the consultation to **qualify** (deadline, branch, scope). When you're near the cap, the DM says "next slots open on __". Scarcity that's true is fine.
- **Track in one Google Sheet:** date · source reel · keyword · consult booked? · paid? · amount. Check it every Sunday.

## 5. Proof without on-camera testimonials (you have screenshots/chats)
1. **Ask permission in writing** (WhatsApp is fine), then blur names/faces/college unless they say yes.
2. Formats:
   - a "DM of the week" story
   - a "What students told us" carousel
   - a screen recording scrolling a real chat, with Shawn reacting on camera
3. **From now on, at handover** ask every team for: a 10-second phone video of their working project + permission. Even a voice note works. Make it part of the delivery checklist.
4. Never edit or invent a quote. A fake one, once it's caught, destroys a trust-based brand.
